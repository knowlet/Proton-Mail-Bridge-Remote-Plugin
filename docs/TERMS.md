## Self-hosted operation

You operate your own Mac, Proton Mail Bridge session, tunnel, credentials, and private ChatGPT connection. The project supplies code and a setup workflow; it does not sell a mailbox service, process payments, or provide a shared hosted mail account.

Use accounts and mail you are authorized to access. Decide which mail to make available to your AI client, protect your local account and credentials, and keep your private mailbox connection private. Read-only access can still disclose confidential content. Drafting and sending require the appropriate local mode and explicit user instructions.
